What We Do

Core Services

Every engagement is backed by firsthand NRC regulatory experience and decades of applied research in nuclear structural integrity.

01
Fracture Mechanics & Flaw Evaluation

Structural integrity assessments, crack initiation and propagation analysis, and flaw tolerance evaluations for passive nuclear components — reactor pressure vessels, internals, steam generators, piping, and other pressure-retaining components.

ASME XI / IWB 3640
02
Probabilistic Fracture Mechanics

PFM analyses supporting long-term operation of existing plants and design support for ASME Section XI Division 2 RIM programs. Experienced lead developer of the xLPR probabilistic fracture mechanics code used throughout the nuclear industry.

xLPR / PRO-LOCA
03
Aging Management Programs

Review and development of aging management programs for passive nuclear components — including irradiation embrittlement, stress corrosion cracking, and environmental degradation — supporting license renewal and extended operations.

LTO / License Renewal
04
Regulatory Advisory & Licensing

Expert guidance through all stages of the NRC licensing process for the operating and new reactor fleet — topical report reviews, relief requests, safety evaluations, rulemaking support, and ASME code compliance.

10 CFR / NRC Process
05
Materials Selection & Qualification

Material selection, qualification, and deployment support for light water and non-light water reactor designs — including advanced reactor concepts, new build projects, and in-service inspection program development.

LWR / Non-LWR Reactors
06
Training & Technical Mentoring

Specialized training in materials science, structural integrity, and aging behaviors for passive nuclear components — drawing on experience leading and mentoring teams of up to 13 engineers at the NRC with a ~$7M annual research budget.
